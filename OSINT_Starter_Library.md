## Quick Review Rubric
- 1 = low value / weak reliability
- 2 = limited use
- 3 = useful with caution
- 4 = strong and useful
- 5 = highly relevant and trustworthy

Recommended decision rules:
- Relevance 4-5 + Reliability 4-5 = Keep and reference
- Relevance 3-4 + Reliability 2-3 = Useful with caution
- Reliability 1-2 = Archive or reject
- Contradicted or unverifiable = follow up before using as source material

  - Data must be verified and contextualized
  - Good OSINT is methodical, not just “search-heavy”
- Main Findings:
  - Bad research often comes from confirmation bias and weak evidence handling
  - Overstating certainty damages trust
  - Documentation and disclosure improve reproducibility
- Methodology Used:

  - Method critique
- Sources Cited:
  - Public examples
  - Investigative case references
- Risks / Limitations:
  - Some lessons are generic rather than domain-specific
- Follow-Up Questions:
  - Which specific workflow errors are most common in our research?
- Legal / Ethical Review:
  - Low risk; educational and methodological
- Tags:
  - osint

  - verification
  - ethics
