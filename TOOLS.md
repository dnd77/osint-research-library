# OSINT Tools and Sources

This file summarizes a practical starter toolkit for OSINT research. The goal is not to provide a complete toolchain, but to give a useful shortlist of reputable, widely used, and relevant sources that can support investigation, research, and public-source collection.

## General-purpose OSINT frameworks

### SpiderFoot
- GitHub: https://github.com/smicallef/spiderfoot
- Description: An open-source intelligence automation and recon framework for footprinting and exposure analysis.
- Best for: domains, companies, infrastructure, public exposure
- Notes: One of the strongest general-purpose options for web footprinting and data gathering.

### OSINT-Framework
- GitHub: https://github.com/lockfale/OSINT-Framework
- Description: A curated index of OSINT tools grouped by type and use case.
- Best for: learning the landscape of available tools and categories
- Notes: Helpful as a reference map rather than a standalone tool.

### sn0int
- GitHub: https://github.com/kpcyrd/sn0int
- Description: A semi-automated OSINT framework for structured collection, reconnaissance, and investigation.
- Best for: building repeatable workflows and modules
- Notes: Useful for more advanced structured intelligence gathering.

## Website and domain discovery

### theHarvester
- GitHub: https://github.com/laramies/theHarvester
- Description: A widely used OSINT tool for collecting emails, subdomains, and other related public data.
- Best for: domain discovery and email-related research
- Notes: A classic tool for early-stage reconnaissance.

### web-check
- GitHub: https://github.com/lissy93/web-check
- Description: A one-stop tool for website intelligence, footprinting, and public exposure review.
- Best for: site analysis and reconnaissance
- Notes: Great for evaluating exposed infrastructure and public web presence.

### Photon
- GitHub: https://github.com/s0md3v/Photon
- Description: A fast crawler for OSINT-style web analysis.
- Best for: website crawling and public content discovery
- Notes: Useful for footprinting and collecting details about a target web presence.

## Social / people intelligence

### Osintgram
- GitHub: https://github.com/Datalux/Osintgram
- Description: An OSINT tool focused on Instagram account analysis and profile data collection.
- Best for: social-media intelligence and SOCMINT scenarios
- Notes: Useful when working with public social media profiles and account data.

### skiptracer
- GitHub: https://github.com/xillwillx/skiptracer
- Description: A Python-based OSINT tool for identifying and correlating profile information.
- Best for: people and account research
- Notes: Useful for identity-related public-source collection.

## Curated OSINT resource lists

### awesome-osint
- GitHub: https://github.com/jivoi/awesome-osint
- Description: A classic and highly cited curated list of OSINT resources.
- Best for: discovery of additional reading, tools, and references
- Notes: One of the best starting points for broad OSINT research.

### Awesome-OSINT-List
- GitHub: https://github.com/Astrosp/Awesome-OSINT-List
- Description: A modern curated list of OSINT tools, categories, and resources.
- Best for: exploring categories and current collections
- Notes: A good complement to the original awesome-osint list.

### tracelabs/awesome-osint
- GitHub: https://github.com/tracelabs/awesome-osint
- Description: Community-curated OSINT tool and resource list.
- Best for: alternative or community-vetted resources
- Notes: Helpful when you want multiple perspectives on the tool ecosystem.

## Research and verification sources

### Bellingcat
- Website: https://www.bellingcat.com/
- Description: High-quality public-source investigation and methodology-driven research.
- Best for: case studies, geolocation, public-source methods
- Notes: Excellent for investigation quality and methodology.

### Recorded Future
- Website: https://www.recordedfuture.com/
- Description: Threat intelligence and cybersecurity intelligence with OSINT-related reporting and methodologies.
- Best for: cybersecurity and threat-intel framing
- Notes: Strong for understanding current intelligence workflows and adversary behavior.

### Citizen Lab
- Website: https://citizenlab.ca/
- Description: Research and investigations focused on digital rights, surveillance, and public-interest investigations.
- Best for: policy, digital accountability, and public-source investigation
- Notes: Useful for broader context beyond pure tooling.

## Suggested starter stack
If you want a compact, practical starter set, begin with:
- SpiderFoot
- theHarvester
- web-check
- OSINT-Framework
- awesome-osint
- Bellingcat
- Recorded Future

This gives a balanced combination of tooling, methodology, and source quality.

## Best practice
These tools are most useful when paired with strong documentation, careful verification, and ethical awareness. Public-source data is only valuable when it is checked, contextualized, and clearly documented.
